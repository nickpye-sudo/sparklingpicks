Is a Soda Maker Worth It?

The real math on costs, savings, and break-even.

📅 Updated Feb 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Short answer: Yes — if you drink sparkling water regularly. A soda maker pays for itself in 3-6 months, then saves you hundreds per year.

  • Homemade sparkling water: ~$0.25 per liter
  • Store-bought: $1.00-2.00 per liter
  • Break-even: 3-6 months (depending on consumption)
  • Annual savings: $150-400+ for regular drinkers

Beyond the Money: Other Benefits

🌍 Environmental Impact

One reusable bottle replaces 2,000+ plastic bottles over its lifetime. Significant reduction in single-use plastic waste.

🏋️ Convenience

No more carrying heavy bottles from the store. Sparkling water on demand, whenever you want it.

✨ Freshness

Always fresh, never flat. Make exactly as much as you need, when you need it.

🎚️ Control

Adjust carbonation to your preference. Light fizz, heavy fizz — whatever you want.

When Is a Soda Maker NOT Worth It?

To be fair, there are scenarios where a soda maker doesn't make sense:

  • You rarely drink sparkling water — Less than 2-3 liters per week makes break-even take over a year
  • You only drink flavored sodas — SodaStream syrups exist but don't match brand-name taste
  • You have limited counter space — Soda makers need a permanent home
  • You prefer specific brands — Nothing matches the exact taste of San Pellegrino or Topo Chico

The Verdict

✅ A Soda Maker Is Worth It If:

  • You drink 3+ liters of sparkling water per week
  • You're tired of buying and carrying bottles
  • You want to reduce plastic waste
  • You value fresh, on-demand carbonation

Our recommendation: The SodaStream Terra ($89.99) pays for itself within months for regular sparkling water drinkers, then saves $300-500+ per year ongoing.
