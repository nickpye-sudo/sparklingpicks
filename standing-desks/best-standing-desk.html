How to Choose a Standing Desk

1. Weight Capacity

Add up your monitors, laptop, books, and accessories. Most people need 50-100 lbs of capacity. Get a desk rated for 250+ lbs for safety margin — you don't want wobble when typing.

2. Desktop Size

  • Single monitor: 48" x 24" minimum
  • Dual monitors: 60" x 30"
  • Triple monitors: 72" x 30"

Don't underestimate depth — 30" gives you comfortable arm positioning with a keyboard tray or monitor arms.

3. Height Range

For sitting: desk surface at elbow height (typically 25-30"). For standing: wrists neutral when typing (typically 38-48"). Tall users (6'2"+) need extended-range frames.

4. Stability

This is where cheap desks fail. Test for wobble at standing height with your hands on the keyboard — any lateral movement is a red flag. C-frame and T-frame designs tend to be more stable than straight columns.

5. Motor Quality

Dual-motor systems are quieter and more reliable than single-motor. Look for:

  • Noise: Under 55 dB (library-quiet)
  • Speed: 1-1.5" per second
  • Anti-collision: Stops if it hits something

6. Warranty

Quality indicator: premium desks offer 10-15 year warranties on frame and motor. Under 5 years = budget build. The motor is the first thing to fail on cheap desks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I spend on a standing desk?

For a quality electric standing desk that will last 10+ years, budget $400-700. Below $400, you sacrifice build quality and stability. Above $700, you're paying for premium materials or brand names, which is worth it if you use your desk 8+ hours daily.

Are standing desks good for your back?

Yes, when used correctly. The key is alternating between sitting and standing — not standing all day. Studies show that switching positions every 30-60 minutes reduces back pain and improves posture. A standing desk makes this easy.

How long do electric standing desks last?

Quality electric standing desks last 10-15 years with normal use. The motors are rated for 10,000+ cycles (up and down). Budget desks may have motor issues after 3-5 years. Look for warranties of 5+ years as a quality indicator.

What size standing desk do I need?

For a single monitor setup: 48" x 24" minimum. For dual monitors: 60" x 30". For triple monitors or heavy workstation: 72" x 30". Depth is often overlooked — 30" depth provides comfortable arm positioning.

Should I get a standing desk converter or a full desk?

If you already have a solid desk and limited budget, a converter ($150-400) can work. But they're clunky, take up desk space, and most people upgrade to a full desk within 2 years. A full desk is a better long-term investment.

How long should I stand at my standing desk?

Start with 15-30 minutes per hour and increase gradually. Most ergonomic experts recommend a 1:1 or 2:1 sitting-to-standing ratio. Listen to your body — fatigue is a signal to sit down.
