Standing Desk Converter Buying Guide

What to Look For

Width: Measure your monitors. Two 24" monitors need at least 35" width. A single monitor or laptop can work with 29-32".

Height Range: You need the keyboard at elbow height when standing. For most people (5'6" to 6'2"), a converter with 17-20" maximum height works. Taller users should verify the max height reaches their elbow height.

Lift Mechanism: Gas-spring is the best value — smooth, effortless, no power needed. Pneumatic (Ergotron) is similar but higher quality. Electric is most convenient but costs more and can fail.

Keyboard Tray: Dual-tier designs with a separate keyboard tray are more ergonomic — your keyboard sits lower than your monitors, keeping your wrists straight. Single-tier designs are simpler but put everything at the same height.

What You Give Up vs. Full Standing Desks

  • Workspace: Converters offer 29-41" of width vs. 48-72" for full desks
  • Depth when collapsed: Converters stick up 6-8" when lowered, reducing your seated desk depth
  • Cable management: Wires move with the converter, which can be messy
  • Stability: Even good converters have slight wobble vs. bolted-down desks

When to Upgrade to a Full Standing Desk

If you find yourself standing 4+ hours daily and want maximum workspace, it's time for a full desk. Our standing desk guide covers the best options from $400-$1,500.

FAQ

Are standing desk converters worth it?

Yes, if you can't or don't want to replace your existing desk. Converters cost $150-500 vs $400-800+ for full standing desks, and you can use them with any desk you already own. They're ideal for renters, people with nice furniture, or anyone wanting to try standing without a big investment.

What's the difference between a converter and a full standing desk?

A standing desk converter sits on top of your existing desk and raises/lowers your keyboard and monitor. A full standing desk replaces your entire desk with an adjustable-height frame. Converters are cheaper and portable but have less surface area. Full desks offer more workspace but require replacing your furniture.

How much workspace do I need for a standing desk converter?

Most converters need 35-40 inches of desk width and 20-24 inches of depth. For dual monitors, choose a 35-inch or wider model. The converter itself will use about 6-8 inches of depth when collapsed, so ensure your desk has at least 24 inches total depth.

Are electric standing desk converters better than manual?

Electric converters offer smoother, one-button adjustment and are easier on your back (no lifting). Manual (gas-spring) converters are more affordable, have no motors to break, and are still quite easy to operate. For most people, a quality gas-spring converter like the FlexiSpot M2B offers the best value.
