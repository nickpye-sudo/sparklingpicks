Frequently Asked Questions

Is a soda maker worth buying?

Yes, if you drink sparkling water regularly. A soda maker pays for itself in 3-6 months compared to buying bottles. See our full cost analysis.

How much does CO2 cost per liter?

A 60L CO2 cylinder costs $15-20 to exchange, making each liter cost about $0.25-0.35. Compare that to $1.50+ for store-bought.

Can I carbonate drinks other than water?

Only with the DrinkMate OmniFizz. SodaStream and Aarke are designed for water only — carbonating sugary drinks can cause overflow and void warranties.
