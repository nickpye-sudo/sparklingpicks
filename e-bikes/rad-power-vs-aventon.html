Rad Power vs Aventon: Which E-Bike Brand Should You Buy?

Updated March 2026 • 12 models tested • Head-to-head comparison

Rad Power Bikes and Aventon are two of the most popular direct-to-consumer e-bike brands in the US. Both offer quality bikes at competitive prices — but they target different riders. After testing multiple models from each brand, here's our complete breakdown.

🏆 Quick Verdict

Aventon wins for most buyers

Aventon bikes are lighter, faster (Class 3), and better for commuting. Rad Power excels in utility bikes (cargo, folding) and has the best customer support in the industry. Choose Aventon for speed and range; choose Rad Power for cargo and versatility.

Motors & Performance

🔧 Motor Comparison

Rad Power standardizes on 750W geared hub motors across nearly all models. This gives consistent torque for hills and cargo — you always know what you're getting.

Aventon varies motor size by model (350W–750W), prioritizing efficiency over raw power on lighter commuter bikes. Their flagship models match Rad's 750W output.

Key difference: Aventon offers Class 3 capability (28 mph pedal assist) on most models. Rad Power limits most bikes to Class 2 (20 mph) but provides more torque for hauling.

Winner: Aventon for speed; Rad Power for torque and hills.

Battery & Range

🔋 Real-World Range Testing

We tested comparable models from each brand under similar conditions (mixed terrain, 170 lb rider, moderate assist):

Aventon Soltera.2 42 miles (rated 46)
Rad Power RadCity 5 35 miles (rated 45)
Aventon Aventure.2 48 miles (rated 60)
Rad Power RadRover 6 32 miles (rated 45)

Aventon consistently delivers 10-15% better real-world range, largely due to lighter weight and more efficient motors.

Winner: Aventon

Build Quality & Components

🔩 Frame & Components

Both brands use 6061 aluminum alloy frames with similar welding quality. Key differences:

  • Brakes: Both use hydraulic disc brakes on mid-tier+ models. Aventon's are slightly more responsive.
  • Drivetrain: Shimano components on both, similar quality tier.
  • Display: Aventon's color LCD displays are cleaner and more readable in sunlight.
  • Tires: Rad Power uses puncture-resistant tires standard; Aventon offers them as upgrades.

Winner: Tie — both are well-built for the price.

Customer Support & Warranty

🛠️ After-Sale Experience

Rad Power wins here decisively:

  • Physical service centers in major cities
  • Mobile repair vans in select areas
  • Same-day phone support
  • 2-year comprehensive warranty
  • Easy parts ordering

Aventon offers standard support:

  • Phone and email support
  • 1-year warranty (2 years on frame)
  • Good parts availability
  • Growing dealer network

If you're not comfortable with basic bike maintenance, Rad Power's support infrastructure is a significant advantage.

Winner: Rad Power
