When a Soda Maker Doesn't Save Money

Skip the soda maker if:

  • You barely drink sparkling water — less than 1L/week means very slow break-even
  • You only drink premium brands occasionally — treating yourself to Perrier once a week is cheaper than owning a machine
  • You prefer flavored varieties — LaCroix/Bubly flavors are hard to replicate exactly

Hidden Savings

The per-liter math doesn't capture everything:

  • No trips to the store — time saved not lugging heavy water bottles
  • No storage space needed — bottles take up pantry/fridge space
  • Never run out — as long as you have water and CO2, you're covered
  • Fresher fizz — make it when you want it, not drink something bottled weeks ago

The Bottom Line

If you drink at least 3 liters of sparkling water per week, a soda maker pays for itself within 6 months and saves hundreds of dollars annually. At 7+ liters/week, you're looking at $400-800 in annual savings.

The math is unambiguous: home carbonation is dramatically cheaper than buying bottles.
