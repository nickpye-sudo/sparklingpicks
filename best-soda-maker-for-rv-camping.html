Best Soda Maker for RV & Camping

Sparkling water off the grid — no outlet needed

📅 Updated Feb 2026 ⏱ 6 min read

Good news: most soda makers are perfect for RV and camping use. They're manual devices — no electricity, no batteries. Just CO2 pressure. Fill a bottle with cold water, push a button, and you have sparkling water in the middle of nowhere.

Here's what to look for and our top picks.

⚡ Quick Answer

SodaStream Terra is the best choice for most RV and camping trips. Lightweight, no power needed, and easy CO2 swaps at any major retailer.

What Matters for RV/Camping

✓ No Electricity Required

This rules out electric models like the SodaStream E-Terra. Stick with manual machines — they work identically anywhere.

✓ Compact & Lightweight

RV counter space is precious. You want the smallest footprint possible. Weight matters less in an RV but more for backpacking.

✓ Durable Construction

Vibration, bumps, and travel. The machine needs to handle being moved regularly.

✓ Easy CO2 Access

Can you get refills on the road? SodaStream cylinders are available at Walmart, Target, Bed Bath & Beyond, and many grocery stores across North America and Europe.

Best Overall: SodaStream Terra

CO2 Refills on the Road

Running out of CO2 is the main concern for extended trips. Here's how to handle it:

SodaStream Cylinder Availability

SodaStream has excellent distribution. You can exchange cylinders at:

  • Walmart (most locations)
  • Target
  • Bed Bath & Beyond
  • Many grocery chains (Kroger, Safeway, etc.)
  • Amazon (buy ahead, ship to campground)

In Europe: major supermarkets, MediaMarkt, and electronics retailers stock SodaStream cylinders.

Pro Tip: Carry Spares

For extended trips, carry 2-3 full cylinders. Each 60L cylinder carbonates ~60 liters of water. For a couple drinking 2L/day, that's about a month per cylinder.

💡 Large Tank Adapter

For full-time RVers, consider a CO2 adapter that connects to larger restaurant-sized tanks (5-20 lbs). Much cheaper per liter and you won't run out. Search "SodaStream external tank adapter" — runs about $30-50.

What to Avoid

✗ Skip These for Camping:

  • SodaStream E-Terra — requires power outlet
  • Aarke Carbonator Pro — glass carafe too fragile for travel
  • Heavy metal machines — unnecessary weight
  • Plumbed-in systems — obviously not portable

RV/Camping Soda Maker Kit

Here's what we pack for a 2-week trip:

  • ✓ SodaStream Terra ($89)
  • ✓ 2× extra bottles ($20)
  • ✓ 2× full CO2 cylinders ($40)
  • ✓ Small cooler space for chilling water

Total: ~$150 — and you'll have sparkling water for the entire trip.

Final Thoughts

The SodaStream Terra is the obvious choice for RV and camping. It's light, reliable, needs no power, and you can find CO2 almost anywhere. The only reason to choose differently is if you specifically want to carbonate cocktails (DrinkMate) or already own a different machine.

For off-grid adventures, there's something satisfying about making fresh sparkling water in the middle of nowhere. Cold water, a few pumps, and you're drinking bubbles by the campfire.
