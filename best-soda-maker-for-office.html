Best Soda Maker for Office

Upgrade your break room from stale bottled water

📅 Updated Feb 2026 ⏱ 7 min read

Office sparkling water makes sense. It's cheaper than stocking bottled water, more sustainable, and people actually enjoy it. But which machine handles the demands of an office environment?

We've tested soda makers in office settings (including our own) and identified the key factors: durability, ease of use for multiple people, and CO2 efficiency. Here are our picks.

⚡ Quick Picks

  • Best Overall: SodaStream Terra — reliable, cheap, everyone can use it
  • Small Office (Personal Desk): SodaStream Terra — compact, no power needed
  • Design-Forward Office: Aarke Carbonator 3 — looks impressive
  • High-Volume Break Room: Consider commercial options

What Matters for Office Use

1. Ease of Use (Critical)

In an office, multiple people use the same machine — many of whom have never touched a soda maker. Simple operation is essential. No instruction manual should be required.

2. Durability

Office equipment gets treated... casually. You want something that handles daily abuse without breaking.

3. CO2 Efficiency

Higher volume = more cylinder swaps. Machines that use CO2 efficiently (or work with larger tanks) save hassle.

4. Multiple Bottles

You'll want 3-4 bottles in rotation so people aren't waiting while one chills.

High-Volume Break Rooms

For offices with 20+ people using sparkling water daily, consumer machines start to struggle. You'll burn through CO2 cylinders weekly and bottles get ratty fast.

Options to Consider:

  • Multiple consumer machines: 2-3 Terras spread across the office. Simple, no single point of failure.
  • Large CO2 tank adapter: Convert a Terra to use restaurant-sized CO2 tanks. More upfront work, but way cheaper per liter.
  • Commercial under-counter system: Brands like Billi or Zip offer plumbed-in sparkling water. Expensive ($2,000+) but truly high-capacity.

💡 The Two-Machine Strategy

For 15-30 people, we recommend two SodaStream Terras in different locations (kitchen + secondary break area). Total cost: ~$380 fully equipped. No waiting, and if one breaks, the other covers.
