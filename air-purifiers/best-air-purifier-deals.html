💡 Air Purifier Deal Tips

  • Check CADR ratings: Match purifier capacity to your room size (coverage = room size × 1.5 for best results)
  • Factor in filter costs: Replacement filters cost $20-60/year. Some brands are pricier than others
  • Look for H13 True HEPA: Captures 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns and larger — medical-grade filtration
  • Spring sales peak: Best deals during March-April (allergy season) and November (Black Friday/holiday)
  • Smart features worth it? App control and air quality sensors add $30-50 but offer real convenience
