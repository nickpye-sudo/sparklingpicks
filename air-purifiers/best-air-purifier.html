What to Look For in an Air Purifier

Room Size & CADR

CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) tells you how quickly a purifier filters air. Match the CADR to your room size — a purifier rated for 200 sq ft won't effectively clean a 400 sq ft living room. When in doubt, size up.

Filter Types

True HEPA: The gold standard. Captures 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns — including dust, pollen, pet dander, and most bacteria.

Activated Carbon: Absorbs odors, VOCs, and smoke. Essential if you're dealing with cooking smells or off-gassing from furniture.

UV-C / Ionizers: Marketing more than performance. Some produce trace ozone. We prefer mechanical filtration.

Running Costs

Filter replacements are the hidden cost. Budget $40-100/year for filters depending on the model. Energy-efficient models (ENERGY STAR certified) cost $20-40/year to run continuously.
