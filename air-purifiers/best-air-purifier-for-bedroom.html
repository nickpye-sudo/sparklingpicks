🛏️ Bedroom Air Purifier Buying Guide

Noise Level is #1

For sleep, noise trumps everything. A powerful purifier that keeps you awake defeats the purpose. Target <35 dB on the lowest setting — that's quieter than a library. Our top picks all hit this threshold.

LED Brightness Matters

Even a small blue LED can be jarring at 3 AM. Look for purifiers with: (1) display-off options, (2) auto-dimming, or (3) no LEDs at all. The Blueair 411a has zero display; the Levoit models let you turn displays off completely.

Sleep Modes Vary Widely

Some "sleep modes" just lower fan speed. Better ones also dim/disable displays, reduce sensor activity, and optimize for overnight energy use. The Coway eco mode and Dyson night mode are the most thoughtful implementations.

Coverage: Match Your Room

Don't overbuy. A 400 sq ft purifier in a 150 sq ft bedroom means you're paying for capacity you'll never use. Our picks range from 183-400 sq ft — measure your bedroom and match accordingly.

Running Overnight: Energy Cost

Most bedroom purifiers use 5-15 watts on low. Running 8 hours/night, that's ~$0.01-0.02/night or $4-8/year in electricity. The bigger ongoing cost is filters: budget $20-70/year depending on model.
