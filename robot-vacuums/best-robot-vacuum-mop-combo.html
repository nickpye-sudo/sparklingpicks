What to Know Before Buying

They're maintenance cleaners, not deep cleaners

Robot vacuum mop combos won't replace your regular vacuum or mop for deep cleaning. Think of them as tireless daily maintenance workers. Run them consistently and they'll keep your floors cleaner than you likely would on your own.

Mop types matter

  • Spinning pads — Two rotating pads that scrub as the robot moves. Good coverage, especially on edges.
  • Vibrating pads — Single pad that vibrates at high frequency. Often removable for vacuum-only mode.
  • Roller mops — Continuously clean themselves during operation, keeping water fresher.
  • Stationary pads — Basic, budget option. Works for light dust but can't handle tough stains.

Dock size varies significantly

Self-cleaning docks with water tanks are convenient but take up space. Budget models often have smaller docks because they don't handle water. Measure your space before buying.
