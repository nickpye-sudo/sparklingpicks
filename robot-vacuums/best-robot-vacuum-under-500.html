Frequently Asked Questions

Is a $400 robot vacuum as good as a $1,000 one?

For basic vacuuming, yes — 80% as good. Premium robots add obstacle avoidance (won't run over cables/pet waste), self-washing mop systems, and better build quality. If you have pets that have accidents or lots of cables on the floor, the premium features matter. For most homes, a $400-500 robot does the job.

Do I need a self-emptying dock?

It's the #1 convenience upgrade. Without it, you need to empty the dustbin every 1-2 cleans. With auto-empty, you might go 4-6 weeks between bag changes. Every robot on this list includes a self-emptying dock.

LiDAR vs camera navigation — what's the difference?

LiDAR uses lasers for precise room mapping — it's more accurate in low light and creates better maps. Camera-based systems (like Roomba's) rely on visual landmarks. Both work well in 2026, but LiDAR is still considered more reliable.

Are Chinese robot vacuum brands reliable?

Roborock and Ecovacs are the #2 and #3 best-selling robot vacuum brands worldwide. They've been making robots since 2014-2016 and have excellent track records. Parts and support are readily available on Amazon.
