Roomba (iRobot) Est. 2002

Roomba invented the robot vacuum category and has been iterating for over 20 years. Their current lineup focuses on intelligent navigation and pet-friendly features, particularly their PrecisionVision camera system that identifies and avoids obstacles like cables, shoes, and pet waste.

✓ Roomba Strengths

  • Best obstacle avoidance in the industry
  • Rubber dual extractors = no hair tangles
  • Mature, polished app with smart home integration
  • Excellent customer support & parts availability
  • Pet Waste Guarantee (j7+ models)

✗ Roomba Weaknesses

  • Premium pricing across the lineup
  • Lower raw suction than competitors
  • Slower cleaning per square foot
  • No built-in mop on most models

Who Should Buy Roomba?

  • Pet owners — The j7+ is the only robot that reliably avoids pet waste, and the rubber extractors never tangle with pet hair
  • Homes with lots of obstacles — Cables, shoes, toys on the floor? Roomba handles it
  • Smart home enthusiasts — Best Alexa/Google integration and automation options
  • People who value polish — The app and overall experience feel more refined

Who Should Buy Shark?

  • Value seekers — Get 80% of Roomba's features at 60% of the price
  • Homes with heavy debris — Stronger suction picks up more on the first pass
  • People who want vacuum + mop — Better combo options than Roomba
  • Allergy sufferers — HEPA filtration on self-empty bases

The Bottom Line

Roomba wins on smarts. If you have pets, lots of obstacles, or want the most "set it and forget it" experience, the j7+ is worth the premium. It simply handles real-world homes better.

Shark wins on value. If you want strong cleaning performance without paying Roomba prices, the Shark AI Ultra delivers. It's the best robot vacuum under $500.

🏆 Our Pick: Roomba j7+ for Most Homes
