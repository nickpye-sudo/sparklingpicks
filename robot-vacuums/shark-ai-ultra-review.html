Quick Verdict

The Shark AI Ultra punches well above its price class. For around $400, you get features that competitors charge $600+ for: a self-emptying base, LiDAR navigation, HEPA filtration, and a self-cleaning brushroll that actually prevents hair tangles.

Is it as refined as a Roomba j7+? No — the app is clunkier, and obstacle avoidance isn't as sophisticated. But for most homes, the Shark AI Ultra delivers 90% of the performance at 65% of the price. If you're pragmatic about value, this is the robot vacuum to buy.

Bottom line: The best robot vacuum for people who want premium features without the premium price tag. Not the absolute best, but the best value.

Navigation & Mapping

Shark uses 360° LiDAR for navigation, the same technology found in premium Roborock models. In our testing, the Shark AI Ultra mapped our 1,800 sq ft test home accurately on the first run — no "learning period" required.

🧪 Navigation Test Results

We ran the Shark AI Ultra in a cluttered living room with furniture, rugs, and a kitchen transition. Results:

  • Room detection: Correctly identified all 6 rooms
  • Furniture avoidance: No collisions with chair legs, sofas, or tables
  • Rug transitions: Handled low-pile rugs well; struggled on thick shag once
  • Return to base: 100% success rate over 30 cleaning sessions

The Matrix Clean pattern means the robot methodically covers every inch — it doesn't just bounce randomly. You can watch it systematically clean row by row, which is satisfying and effective.

Where it falls short: Unlike the Roomba j7+, the Shark won't identify and avoid small obstacles like charging cables, pet toys, or socks. If your floors tend to be cluttered, expect occasional tangles or stuck situations.

Cleaning Performance

At 2,500Pa, the Shark AI Ultra has stronger suction than the Roomba j7+ (2,200Pa). In practice, this means better pickup on carpets and embedded dirt on hard floors.

🧪 Our Cleaning Tests

Hard floor (rice/cereal test): 98% pickup in one pass. Excellent.

Low-pile carpet (baking soda test): 92% pickup. Strong performance.

Medium carpet (pet hair test): 89% pickup. Self-cleaning brush handled fur well.

Edge cleaning: Good but not great — round shape misses some corners (the D-shaped Roomba s9+ does better here).

For everyday dust, dirt, and pet hair, the Shark AI Ultra performs admirably. It's not the absolute best (the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra edges it out), but it's competitive with robots costing $200+ more.

Self-Cleaning Brushroll

This is a genuinely useful feature. The brushroll has a comb built into the guard that actively removes hair as the robot operates. After 6 months of testing in a home with two dogs, we've never had to manually clean hair tangles.

Compare this to the Roomba j7+'s rubber extractors (also good) or cheaper robots where you're picking out hair weekly. The self-cleaning brushroll is a real time-saver for pet owners.

Self-Empty Base with HEPA

The bagless self-empty base is a standout feature at this price point. Key advantages:

  • Bagless design — no ongoing bag costs (unlike Roomba's $20/3-pack bags)
  • HEPA filtration — traps 99.97% of dust, pollen, and allergens
  • 60-day capacity — truly hands-off operation
  • Easy to empty — pop open, dump, close. Takes 30 seconds.

The emptying process is loud (~75dB for about 10 seconds), but that's standard for self-empty bases. We recommend scheduling cleans when you're away.

SharkClean App

The SharkClean app gets the job done, but it's not as polished as iRobot's offering. Here's what works and what doesn't:

✓ App Strengths

  • Accurate mapping with room labels
  • Schedule by room or whole-home
  • Suction power controls (quiet/normal/max)
  • Cleaning history and runtime tracking
  • Alexa/Google voice commands work well

✗ App Weaknesses

  • Occasional connection drops
  • Map editing is clunky
  • No-go zones work but setup is tedious
  • Limited smart home integrations vs. iRobot

If app quality is a top priority, the Roomba j7+ is better. But for basic scheduling, room selection, and monitoring, the SharkClean app is adequate.
