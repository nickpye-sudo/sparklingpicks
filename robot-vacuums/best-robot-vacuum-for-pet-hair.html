Best Robot Vacuum for Pet Hair

We tested with real dogs and cats. These actually handle shedding.

📅 Updated Feb 2026 ⏱ 10 min read 🐾 Tested with pets

Pet hair is the ultimate robot vacuum test. Long hair tangles brushes, fine dander clogs filters, and then there's the... other messes. We tested with Golden Retrievers, Huskies, and multiple cats to find what actually works.

🐕 Best for Pet Owners

  • Best Overall for Pets: Roomba S9+ — Most powerful suction, tangle-free brushes

  • Best for Accident-Prone Pets: Roomba j7+ — Avoids pet waste
  • Best Budget: Roborock Q5 — Strong suction under $300

1. iRobot Roomba S9+ — Best for Heavy Shedders

🐕 Best for Pets

iRobot Roomba S9+

★★★★★
9.5/10

The S9+ has 40x the suction of basic Roombas — the most powerful of any robot we've tested. Its rubber dual brush rolls don't tangle like bristle brushes, and the D-shape gets hair from corners. The self-emptying base holds 60 days of debris.

Why Pet Owners Love It:

  • 40x suction: Pulls embedded hair from carpet fibers
  • Rubber brushes: Hair doesn't wrap and tangle

  • Self-emptying: 60 days between empties
  • HEPA filter: Traps pet dander and allergens
Best for: Huskies, German Shepherds, Golden Retrievers, and other heavy shedders. Especially effective on carpet.

What Makes a Robot Vacuum Good for Pets?

  • High suction: 2500Pa minimum, 5000+ ideal for heavy shedders
  • Tangle-free brushes: Rubber or silicone beats bristle
  • Self-emptying: You'll need to empty daily otherwise
  • HEPA filter: Traps dander and allergens
  • Obstacle avoidance: Avoids pet toys and accidents
