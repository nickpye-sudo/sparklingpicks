💡 The Multi-Story Strategy

One robot, one dock, multiple floors works — if you pick the right robot. The best multi-story robot vacuums save 3-4 separate floor maps and recognize which floor they're on automatically. You carry the robot upstairs; it knows where it is. No re-mapping, no confusion. Below, we cover which robots do this best and when you might actually want 2+ robots instead.

How We Tested

We tested each robot vacuum in a 3-story townhome over 2 weeks, evaluating:

  • Multi-floor mapping: How many maps can it save? Does it auto-recognize floors?
  • Floor transition: How accurately does it identify which floor it's on after being moved?
  • Battery life: Can it clean 1,000+ sq ft on a single charge (one full floor)?
  • Portability: Weight and handle design for carrying between floors
  • Dock flexibility: Does it work well without a dock on secondary floors?

What to Look For

🗺️ Multi-Floor Mapping is Non-Negotiable

The #1 feature for multi-story homes is multi-floor map storage. Without it, your robot will re-learn your upstairs layout every single time you move it — wasting 30+ minutes on exploration runs instead of cleaning.

Minimum: 3 saved maps. Ideal: 4+ maps (allows seasonal furniture changes).

Multi-Floor Strategies

🎯 Strategy 1: One Robot, One Dock (Recommended)

Best for: 2-story homes up to 3,000 sq ft total

  • Keep dock on main living floor (usually ground floor)
  • Carry robot to upper floor(s) as needed
  • Run without dock upstairs — just empty dustbin manually after
  • Move robot back to dock when done for charging + self-empty

Why it works: Modern robots with multi-floor mapping handle this seamlessly. You're not wasting money on duplicate hardware, and the robot stays charged by returning to its dock daily.

🎯 Strategy 2: Two Robots (Large Homes)

Best for: 3+ story homes OR floors over 2,000 sq ft each

  • Dedicated robot per 1-2 floors
  • Each robot has its own dock — no carrying required
  • Can run simultaneously for faster whole-home cleaning
  • Higher upfront cost, but more convenience

Cost-effective combo: Premium robot on main floor (Roborock S8 Pro Ultra), budget robot on upper floors (Shark AI Ultra). Total ~$1,800 for full coverage.

What About Stairs?

No robot vacuum can clean stairs — they use cliff sensors to avoid falling. For stairs, you'll need a handheld vacuum or traditional stick vacuum. Popular stair-cleaning options:

  • Dyson V15 Detect — Premium cordless, powerful suction
  • Shark Pet Cordless — Great value, good for pet hair on carpet stairs
  • Black+Decker Dustbuster — Budget handheld for quick stair touch-ups

Many multi-story homeowners keep a cordless stick vacuum at the top or bottom of stairs for quick passes between robot cleanings.

Our Verdict

For most multi-story homes, the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra is our top recommendation. Its combination of 4 floor maps, 180-minute runtime, and excellent navigation makes managing 2-3 floors simple. Yes, it's $1,399 — but it's genuinely a "set and forget" solution that handles the complexity of multi-story living.

If budget is a concern, the Roomba j7+ at $599 offers excellent multi-floor mapping (10 maps!) with slightly shorter runtime. The Shark AI Ultra at $399 is the best budget option if you can live with 3 floor maps.
