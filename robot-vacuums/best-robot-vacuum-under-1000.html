How We Tested & Chose

Real-World Testing

We ran each robot for 4+ weeks in homes with hardwood, tile, carpet, and LVP flooring. Pet hair, Cheerios, and coffee grounds became our test debris. We tracked cleaning coverage, obstacle hits, and dock reliability.

What Matters at $600-1,000

Dock quality: The dock is half the product at this price. All-in-one docks should wash mops with clean water (not recirculated), dry them properly (hot air beats passive), and last 2+ weeks between tank refills.

Obstacle avoidance: AI camera systems beat basic bump-and-go dramatically. If you have kids, pets, or cables, this matters more than suction numbers.

Mopping effectiveness: Rotating mops scrub better than vibrating or passive pads. Mop lift (raising mops when on carpet) prevents wet carpet disasters.

Who Should Spend $600-1,000

This price range makes sense if: (1) you have hard floors that need mopping, (2) you want minimal maintenance with auto-empty docks, or (3) you have pets/kids and need obstacle avoidance. If you're carpet-only and don't mind emptying dustbins, a sub-$500 robot will clean just as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a robot vacuum under $1000 worth it over a $500 model?

Yes, if you want all-in-one docks that auto-empty, wash, and dry mop pads. The $500-1000 range gives you hot-water mopping, better obstacle avoidance (AI cameras vs basic bumpers), and significantly less maintenance. For pet owners or large homes, the convenience upgrade is worth it.

What's the best robot vacuum under $1000 with a self-emptying dock?

The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra ($999) includes a self-emptying dock plus mop washing and drying. If you only need vacuuming (no mop), the Roomba j7+ ($699) has the best self-empty dock reliability.

Which robot vacuum under $1000 is best for pet hair?

The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra or Dreame L10s Ultra have anti-tangle rubber brushes designed for pet hair. Both prevent hair wrap and empty directly into the dock. For iRobot fans, the Roomba j7+ combo specifically handles pet messes with "Pet Owner Official Promise" guarantee.

Do I need a robot vacuum with mop function?

If you have hardwood, tile, or LVP floors, a vacuum-mop combo eliminates a separate mopping step. The best under-$1000 combos have pressure-based mopping (Roborock, Dreame) that scrubs rather than just dragging a wet pad. Skip the mop if you're carpet-only.

Should I wait for a sale?

Yes. Robot vacuums in this price range routinely drop 20-30% during Amazon Prime Day, Black Friday, and holiday sales. The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra hits $799, the Q Revo drops to $499, and Ecovacs X1 Omni goes under $700. Set price alerts.
