Choose Roborock for more features per dollar, mopping capability, and customization.

Where Roomba Wins

1. Obstacle Avoidance

Roomba's PrecisionVision technology (in j7 and newer) is the best we've tested. It identifies and avoids pet waste, cables, socks, shoes, and other floor obstacles. Roborock's 3D obstacle avoidance is good but not as reliable.

2. Brand Reliability & Support

iRobot has been making robot vacuums since 2002. They have US-based customer support, widely available replacement parts, and a proven track record. Roborock is solid but younger.

3. Simplicity

Roomba's app is cleaner and simpler. If you just want a robot that cleans without much configuration, Roomba is more approachable.

Where Roborock Wins

1. Mopping

Roborock integrates vacuuming and mopping into one unit. The S8 Pro Ultra even washes its own mop. Roomba requires a separate Braava Jet robot for mopping — twice the cost and space.

2. Value

At similar price points, Roborock typically offers more features. The S8 Pro Ultra ($1,399) includes vacuum, mop, self-empty, self-wash, and auto-refill. A comparable Roomba setup costs $1,500+.

3. Navigation Precision

Roborock's LiDAR navigation creates more accurate maps than Roomba's camera-based system. The maps are editable, zones are customizable, and the path planning is more efficient.

Our Recommendations

Choose Roomba If:

  • You have pets that might leave "surprises"
  • You want proven reliability and excellent support
  • You prefer simplicity over features
  • You don't need mopping

Choose Roborock If:

  • You want vacuum AND mopping in one unit
  • Value for money is important
  • You want detailed map customization
  • You're comfortable with more settings

The Bottom Line

Both brands make excellent robot vacuums. Roomba is the safer, simpler choice with the best obstacle avoidance. Roborock offers more features per dollar, especially if you want mopping.

Our specific picks:

  • Best Roomba: Roomba j7+ ($799)
  • Best Roborock: S8 Pro Ultra ($1,399)
  • Best Budget: Roborock Q5 ($279)
