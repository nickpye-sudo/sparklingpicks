Buying Guide: What to Expect Under $300

Navigation: LiDAR vs. Gyro vs. Random

LiDAR (Roborock Q5, Ecovacs N8) maps your home with a laser and cleans in efficient lines. It's the gold standard — you get it in the Roborock Q5 at $279, which is excellent value.

Gyro navigation (Roomba, Eufy, Shark) uses motion sensors to track movement. It's more methodical than random but can't build true maps or remember rooms.

Random bounce (iLife) just bumps around until it eventually covers the space. Works for small areas but wastes time in larger homes.

Suction Power: How Much Do You Need?

For hard floors and light dust: 1,000-1,500Pa is fine. For pet hair, carpets, or heavy traffic: look for 2,000Pa+. The Roborock Q5's 2,700Pa handles most situations.

Self-Empty vs. Manual Empty

Most robots under $300 require you to empty the dustbin after each run. The Shark AV2501S is the exception — its self-empty dock is included at $299. If you hate touching dustbins, that's your pick (or budget $400+ for a Roborock Q5+).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a $200 robot vacuum worth it?

Yes — if your expectations are realistic. Budget robots like the Eufy 11S ($179) or Roborock Q5 ($279) clean well on hard floors and low-pile carpet. They won't deep-clean shag carpet or avoid every obstacle, but for daily maintenance, they're excellent value.

Do cheap robot vacuums work on carpet?

On low-pile carpet, yes. The Roborock Q5 (2,700Pa) handles most carpets well. Thicker carpet requires more suction — the Eufy 11S will struggle, while the Roomba 694 compensates with its rubber brushroll design.

How often should I run a budget robot vacuum?

Daily or every other day. Budget robots are maintenance cleaners — they keep floors tidy between deep cleans. Running frequently prevents dirt buildup that overwhelms their smaller dustbins.

Can I use a robot vacuum if I have pets?

Absolutely. The Roborock Q5 and Ecovacs N8 handle pet hair well. For an ultra-budget option designed for pet hair, the iLife V3s Pro ($119) has a tangle-free design specifically for this.
