Why Carpet Requires Different Features

Not all robot vacuums handle carpet equally. Here's what matters most:

  1. Suction power (measured in Pa): Higher Pa ratings pull debris from deeper in carpet fibers. Budget vacuums (2,000-3,000 Pa) handle low-pile; thick carpet needs 5,000+ Pa.
  2. Brush type: Rubber extractors (like Roomba's) agitate carpet fibers and resist hair tangles. Bristle brushes work but require more maintenance.
  3. Auto carpet boost: Good vacuums detect carpet and automatically increase suction, preserving battery on hard floors.
  4. Height adjustment: Some vacuums adjust their height for thick pile. Without this, they may struggle or get stuck.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can robot vacuums clean thick carpet?

Yes, but suction power matters significantly. Premium vacuums with 6,000+ Pa suction (Roomba S9+, Ecovacs X2 Omni, Roborock S8) handle thick and plush carpet well. Budget models may struggle or get stuck on very thick shag carpet.

Do robot vacuums work on shag carpet?

With limitations. Most robot vacuums can clean low-to-medium shag (pile under 1 inch). Very long shag (1.5+ inches) can trap wheels or tangle brushes. The Roomba S9+ and Ecovacs X2 performed best in our shag tests, but we recommend setting no-go zones for very thick areas.

What's the best suction power for carpet?

For low-pile carpet, 3,000+ Pa is sufficient. Medium pile benefits from 5,000+ Pa. Thick carpet and shag need 6,000+ Pa for deep cleaning. The Ecovacs X2 Omni's 8,000 Pa is the strongest we've tested.

Should I get a robot vacuum with rubber or bristle brushes?

Rubber brushes (like Roomba's dual extractors) are better for homes with pets or long-haired occupants—they resist tangles and are easier to clean. Bristle brushes can be slightly more effective at agitating carpet fibers but require more maintenance.

Do I need carpet boost mode?

If you have mixed flooring (carpet + hardwood), yes. Carpet boost automatically increases suction on carpet and reduces it on hard floors, optimizing cleaning power while preserving battery life. All premium vacuums we tested include this feature.
