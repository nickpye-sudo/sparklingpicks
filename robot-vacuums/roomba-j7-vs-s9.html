The Key Difference: Intelligence vs Power

The j7+ and S9+ represent two different philosophies from iRobot:

Roomba j7+: Smart Avoidance

The j7+ uses a front-facing camera with AI to identify and avoid obstacles like pet waste, cables, socks, and shoes. It's the only robot vacuum with iRobot's "P.O.O.P." (Pet Owner Official Promise) guarantee — they'll replace your robot if it runs over pet waste.

This makes the j7+ ideal for homes with pets, kids, or just lots of stuff on the floor.

Roomba S9+: Raw Power

The S9+ has 4x the suction of the j7+ (40x base vs 10x). Its D-shaped design gets into corners that round robots miss. For homes with heavy-shedding pets or lots of carpet, the S9+ cleans more thoroughly.

But it lacks the j7+'s smart obstacle avoidance — it'll barrel through cables and potentially spread pet accidents.

Who Should Buy Which?

Choose Roomba j7+ ($799) If:

  • You have pets (especially not-yet-housetrained ones)
  • You have cables, shoes, or toys on floors
  • You want truly hands-off operation
  • You have mostly hard floors or low-pile carpet
  • Price matters ($200 savings)

Choose Roomba S9+ ($999) If:

  • You have heavy-shedding pets (huskies, German shepherds)
  • You have lots of carpet, especially high-pile
  • You keep floors clear of obstacles
  • Corner cleaning is important to you
  • You want the absolute best suction

🏆 Our Verdict

For most people, we recommend the Roomba j7+. The obstacle avoidance is genuinely useful — no more untangling robots from cables or cleaning up smeared pet messes. And for typical homes, the 10x suction is plenty.

The S9+ is the better choice only if you specifically need maximum suction for heavy pet hair on carpet, and you're confident your floors will be obstacle-free.
