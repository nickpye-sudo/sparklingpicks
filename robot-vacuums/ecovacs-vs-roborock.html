Cleaning Performance

Both robots deliver excellent vacuuming, but they take different approaches:

🟦 Ecovacs X2 Omni

Suction: 8,000 Pa — the highest we've tested. Pulls up embedded dirt and pet hair effortlessly.

Edge Cleaning: The square design is the real advantage here. It gets right into corners where round robots leave debris. We measured 35% better corner coverage in our testing.

Carpet Performance: Excellent on low-pile, good on medium. Auto-boosts suction when detecting carpet.

🟢 Roborock S8 Pro Ultra (Winner)

Suction: 6,000 Pa — slightly less raw power, but the dual rubber brush roller compensates. It's better at grabbing tangled hair.

Edge Cleaning: The extendable side brush reaches edges well, compensating for the round shape. Corners still trail the X2, but mid-room cleaning is marginally better.

Carpet Performance: Superior on medium-pile carpets. The rubber rollers don't tangle like the X2's bristle-hybrid brush.

Our take: If you have a lot of corners and hard floors, the X2 Omni's square design makes a real difference. For homes with more carpets and pets that shed, the S8 Pro Ultra's rubber brush system wins.

Mopping Performance

Both are 2-in-1 vacuum/mop combos with self-washing docks. Here's how they compare:

🟦 Ecovacs X2 Omni

System: Dual rotating mop pads at 180 RPM. Good downward pressure.

Stain Removal: Handles dried coffee and light sticky messes. Struggles with dried sauce.

Hot Water: Uses heated water for washing mop pads — a nice touch that reduces odors.

Lift Height: 15mm — adequate for most low-pile rugs.

🟢 Roborock S8 Pro Ultra (Winner)

System: VibraRise 2.0 sonic mopping. Vibrates 3,000 times/minute with consistent pressure.

Stain Removal: Outperforms the X2 on stubborn stains. The sonic vibration breaks up dried spills better than rotation alone.

Mop Lift: 20mm — clears higher-pile rugs and thick transitions.

Auto-Detection: Carpet detection is more reliable. We saw fewer instances of wet mop touching carpet.

Our take: If mopping is a priority (hard floors throughout, pets, kids), the S8 Pro Ultra's sonic mopping delivers noticeably cleaner floors. The X2's hot water washing is nice, but doesn't compensate for weaker scrubbing.

Navigation & Mapping

🟢 Ecovacs X2 Omni (Winner for Object Avoidance)

Tech: LiDAR + AIVI 3D (structured light camera). The 3D sensing is excellent at spotting obstacles.

Object Avoidance: Best-in-class. Detects cables, socks, pet toys, and shoes reliably. We deliberately left hazards and it dodged everything.

Mapping: Fast initial mapping (~15 min for 1,500 sqft). Multi-floor support works well.

🟢 Roborock S8 Pro Ultra (Winner for Efficiency)

Tech: LiDAR + Reactive 3D obstacle avoidance (RGB + structured light).

Object Avoidance: Very good, but slightly less reliable than X2 for small dark objects.

Mapping: More efficient routes. Completes the same floor plan 10-15% faster. Better at room recognition and edge detection.

Our take: The X2 Omni has marginally better obstacle detection, but the S8 Pro Ultra's smarter routing means faster cleanings and better battery efficiency. For most users, the S8 wins.

App & Smart Features

🟢 Roborock App (Winner)

  • Clean, intuitive interface
  • Detailed 3D mapping with furniture placement
  • Per-room suction and mop settings
  • Reliable scheduling and routines
  • Voice assistant integration (Alexa, Google, Siri Shortcuts)
  • Quick updates, responsive support

🟡 Ecovacs App (Weaker)

  • More cluttered interface
  • Occasional connectivity drops
  • Same features, less polish
  • AIVI video monitoring (privacy concern for some)
  • Slower firmware updates
  • Voice commands work but less reliable

Our take: Roborock's app is simply better designed. You'll interact with it daily, so this matters more than specs suggest.

Better mopping, quieter operation, smarter navigation, superior app — and $100 less. The X2 Omni's square design and object avoidance are impressive, but don't outweigh the S8's overall polish.
