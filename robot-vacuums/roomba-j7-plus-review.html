Obstacle Avoidance: The j7+'s Killer Feature

This is where the j7+ genuinely shines — and why it's our #1 pick despite competitors having stronger suction.

🧪 Our Obstacle Test

We scattered our test floor with: charging cables, dog toys, a sock, sandals, headphones, and (yes) fake pet waste. The j7+ identified and avoided every single obstacle. It even sent us photo alerts: "I found something that looks like a cord — should I avoid this area?"

Competitor comparison: The Roborock S8 ran over the cables. The Shark got tangled in the headphones. The j7+ navigated around everything.

The PrecisionVision camera looks ahead as it cleans, using machine learning to identify common obstacles. This isn't a gimmick — after 8 months, we've had zero incidents of the j7+ getting stuck or spreading pet accidents (something that happened twice with other vacuums during our testing).

The P.O.O.P. (Pet Owner Official Promise) is real: if your j7+ runs over pet waste, iRobot will replace it. The fact they're confident enough to offer this tells you how well the avoidance works.

Cleaning Performance

On paper, the j7+'s 2200Pa suction looks weak compared to the 6000Pa Roborock S8. In reality? It cleans just as well for typical household dirt.

🧪 Our Cleaning Tests

  • Hardwood: Excellent. Picks up dust, crumbs, and debris on first pass.
  • Low-pile carpet: Excellent. Dual rubber brushes pull dirt out effectively.
  • High-pile carpet: Good. Manages well, but thick shag carpets may need the Power Boost mode.
  • Edge cleaning: Very good. Gets within ~5mm of walls.
  • Corners: Good. Not perfect (nothing is), but better than circular competitors.

The dual rubber extractors are a highlight — they don't tangle with hair like bristle brushes do. After 8 months, we've never had to cut hair out of the brushes.

Pet Hair Performance

We test all robot vacuums in homes with pets. The j7+ handles pet hair excellently:

  • Pickup: Collects pet hair from all floor types in 1-2 passes
  • Tangle-free: Rubber brushes don't wrap hair — huge quality-of-life benefit
  • Self-emptying: Clean Base handles pet hair without clogging
  • Allergen filter: High-Efficiency Filter captures 99% of allergens

Self-Emptying Clean Base

The Clean Base is what makes the j7+ truly hands-free. After each clean, the vacuum docks and empties its bin into the base's bag. In our testing, one bag lasted about 60 days with daily cleaning.

Pros:

  • Genuinely hands-free — we went 2 months without touching it
  • AllergenLock bags seal dust and allergens inside
  • Emptying is quick (~10 seconds) and reasonably quiet

Cons:

  • Proprietary bags ($20 for 3-pack) add ~$7/month to running costs
  • Base is larger than competitors (12" × 15" footprint)

iRobot App & Smart Features

The iRobot Home app is one of the best in the industry. It's stable, intuitive, and actually useful.

Key features:

  • Smart Maps: View, label, and set room-specific schedules
  • Clean While I'm Away: Uses phone location to start cleaning when you leave
  • Obstacle photos: Get alerts with images of what the j7+ found
  • Seasonal suggestions: Recommends more frequent cleaning during pollen season
  • Voice control: "Alexa, clean the kitchen" — actually works

Unlike some competitors, we never experienced app crashes, disconnections, or "failed to connect" errors. Setup took under 10 minutes.
