Who We Are

Sparkling Picks is an independent review site helping you find the right soda maker. We test products, compare prices, and provide honest recommendations.

Our Mission

To save you time and money by doing the research so you don't have to. Every recommendation is based on real-world testing and genuine customer feedback.

How We Make Money

Sparkling Picks is funded through affiliate partnerships with Amazon and other retailers. When you click our links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Important: Affiliate relationships never influence our recommendations. We recommend products based on quality and value, not commission rates.

Editorial Standards

  • We purchase products ourselves for testing
  • We don't accept payment for reviews
  • We update content regularly to reflect current prices and availability
  • We clearly disclose affiliate relationships
