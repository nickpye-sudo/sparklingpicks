Soda Maker Troubleshooting

Fix common problems with SodaStream, Aarke, and other soda makers.

📅 Updated Feb 2026 ⏱ 8 min read

Soda maker not working properly? Most problems have simple fixes. This guide covers the most common issues and how to solve them.

Common Problems

  • Weak or No Carbonation
  • Sparkling Water Goes Flat Quickly
  • CO2 Leaking / Hissing Sound
  • Bottle Stuck or Won't Lock
  • Water Overflowing When Carbonating
  • Water Tastes Bad
  • Cylinder Won't Fit

Weak or No Carbonation

Symptoms: Water barely fizzy, or no carbonation at all despite pressing the button.

Possible Causes & Fixes

  1. CO2 cylinder empty — The most common cause. Swap for a full cylinder.
  2. Cylinder not connected properly — Remove and reinsert. For quick-connect, push until you hear a click. For screw-in, tighten fully.
  3. Water too warm — Cold water absorbs CO2 much better. Refrigerate before carbonating.
  4. Bottle not locked — Ensure the bottle is fully twisted into place.
  5. Not pressing long enough — Hold the button for 2-3 seconds per press, not just a quick tap.

Quick Test

Remove the bottle and press the carbonation button. You should hear a strong hiss of CO2. If no sound or very weak, your cylinder is likely empty or not connected properly.

Sparkling Water Goes Flat Quickly

Symptoms: Water is fizzy right after carbonating but loses carbonation within hours.

Possible Causes & Fixes

  1. Not refrigerating immediately — CO2 escapes faster from warm water. Refrigerate right after carbonating.
  2. Cap not sealing properly — Check the cap seal for cracks or wear. Replace if damaged.
  3. Old bottle — SodaStream recommends replacing bottles every 3 years. Old bottles may not seal as well.
  4. Opening frequently — Each time you open the bottle, CO2 escapes. Pour what you need and close quickly.

Pro Tip

For maximum fizz retention: carbonate cold water, keep it refrigerated, and consider using smaller bottles if you don't drink a full liter quickly.

CO2 Leaking / Hissing Sound

Symptoms: Continuous hissing from the machine, cylinder empties faster than normal, or CO2 smell when not in use.

Possible Causes & Fixes

  1. Cylinder not fully connected — Most common cause. Remove and reconnect firmly.
  2. Cross-threaded cylinder (screw-in only) — Unscrew completely and realign before screwing back in.
  3. Faulty cylinder valve — Some cylinders have manufacturing defects. Exchange for a new one.
  4. Damaged machine seal — Less common, but seals can wear out. Contact manufacturer for replacement parts.

Safety note: CO2 is heavier than air. If you smell a significant leak, ventilate the room and remove the cylinder.

Bottle Stuck or Won't Lock

Symptoms: Bottle won't twist into place, or won't release after carbonating.

Possible Causes & Fixes

  1. Wrong bottle type — Ensure you're using compatible bottles. Quick-connect machines need specific bottles.
  2. Overfilled bottle — Fill only to the marked line. Overfilling creates excess pressure.
  3. Residual pressure — If stuck after carbonating, wait 10-15 seconds for pressure to equalize, then try again.
  4. Debris in the lock — Clean the bottle neck and machine connection point.
  5. Damaged threading — Inspect bottle neck for cracks or damage. Replace if worn.

Water Overflowing When Carbonating

Symptoms: Water shoots up and out during carbonation, making a mess.

Possible Causes & Fixes

  1. Overfilled bottle — Never fill above the max line. The water expands when carbonated.
  2. Carbonating too fast — Use slower, controlled presses rather than rapid pressing.
  3. Carbonating non-water (SodaStream/Aarke) — These machines are water-only. Juice or soda will foam violently.
  4. Very cold water + aggressive carbonation — Extremely cold water can cause more vigorous carbonation. Use gentler presses.

Water Tastes Bad

Symptoms: Metallic, plastic, or off-flavor in carbonated water.

Possible Causes & Fixes

  1. New bottle taste — New plastic bottles can have a slight taste. Wash thoroughly before first use and carbonate/discard a few bottles.
  2. Dirty bottle — Bottles need regular cleaning. Dishwasher or hot soapy water.
  3. Old bottle — Plastic degrades over time. Replace bottles every 2-3 years.
  4. Source water quality — If your tap water tastes bad, so will your sparkling water. Try filtered water.
  5. Residue from flavors — If you've added syrups, residue can linger. Clean thoroughly.

Cylinder Won't Fit

Symptoms: New cylinder doesn't fit or connect to your machine.

⚠️ Important: Cylinder Compatibility

SodaStream uses TWO different cylinder types:

  • Quick-connect (pink cap) — For Terra, Art, Duo
  • Screw-in (blue cap) — For Spirit, Source, Aarke

They are NOT interchangeable. Check which type your machine uses before buying.

When to Contact Support

Most problems have DIY fixes, but contact the manufacturer if:

  • Machine makes unusual sounds (grinding, clicking)
  • Visible cracks or damage to the machine body
  • Problems persist after trying all troubleshooting steps
  • Machine is within warranty period (usually 2 years)

Support Contacts

  • SodaStream: sodastream.com/support
  • Aarke: aarke.com/support

Preventing Problems

  • Use cold water: Better carbonation, less strain on the system
  • Don't overfill: Always respect the max line
  • Clean bottles regularly: Weekly for heavy use
  • Replace bottles every 2-3 years
  • Store cylinders upright: Prevents valve issues
  • Keep machine clean: Wipe down regularly, especially the bottle connection area
