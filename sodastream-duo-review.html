Glass vs Plastic: Why It Matters

The Duo exists for people who don't want plastic bottles. Maybe you're concerned about microplastics, maybe you dislike the look and feel of plastic, or maybe you want to serve sparkling water in a carafe at dinner parties.

Arguments for Glass

  • No plastic touching your water — peace of mind for some users
  • More elegant — the glass carafe looks restaurant-quality
  • Better taste (subjective) — some people claim water tastes cleaner from glass
  • Dishwasher-safe — easier to clean thoroughly
  • Longer lifespan — doesn't degrade like plastic (SodaStream recommends replacing plastic bottles every 3 years)

Arguments Against Glass

  • Heavy — the glass carafe weighs about 700g empty, 1.7kg full
  • Breakable — drop it on tile and you're buying a new one ($30+)
  • No fridge storage — the carafe shouldn't be stored under pressure; carbonate, pour, drink
  • More expensive — machine costs $80 more than Terra

💡 Important Note on Glass Bottles

SodaStream recommends not storing carbonated water in the glass carafe. Unlike plastic bottles designed to hold pressure, the glass carafe is meant for immediate use: carbonate, serve, done. If you want to make water in advance and refrigerate it, use the included plastic bottle.

The Bottles: Glass & Plastic

The Duo comes with both bottle types:

Glass Carafe

  • 1 liter capacity
  • ~700g empty
  • Dishwasher-safe
  • Metal cap with seal
  • Not for fridge storage

Plastic Bottle

  • 1 liter capacity
  • ~150g empty
  • Dishwasher-safe
  • BPA-free PET
  • OK for fridge storage

How most people use it: Glass carafe for dinner parties and entertaining, plastic bottle for everyday use and making batches to refrigerate.

Carbonation Performance

Carbonation is identical to the Terra — same mechanism, same results. You're not getting better sparkling water from the Duo; you're getting the same sparkling water in different containers.

The glass carafe does hold fizz slightly differently than plastic — some users report the carbonation feels "sharper" from glass, though this might be placebo. Either way, the carbonation quality is excellent.

Design & Build Quality

The Duo has a more premium feel than the Terra. The body is metal rather than plastic, and the overall aesthetic is more refined. It's not as striking as the Aarke, but it's clearly a step up from the entry-level SodaStream models.

The main design consideration is size — the Duo is taller and heavier than the Terra, and it needs more counter space. If your kitchen is cramped, this might matter.
