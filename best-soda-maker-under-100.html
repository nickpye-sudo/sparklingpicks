Spirit vs Terra

  • Price difference: Spirit saves $20-40 upfront
  • CO2 cost: Spirit uses screw-in ($15-17 refills) vs Terra quick-connect ($17-20)
  • Convenience: Terra is much easier to use

Our verdict: Spend the extra $20-40 for the Terra. The quick-connect CO2 is a meaningful quality-of-life improvement, and the Terra will feel current for years.

What to Avoid Under $100
