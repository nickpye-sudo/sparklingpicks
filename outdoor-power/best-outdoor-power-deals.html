💡 Tips for Finding the Best Outdoor Power Deals

  • Spring is prime time — Lawn equipment goes on sale March-May for spring yard prep
  • Buy snow blowers in summer — Off-season prices are 15-25% lower
  • Stick to one ecosystem — Buy tools from the same brand to share batteries and save money
  • Check bundles — Battery + charger kits often cost less than buying separately
  • Memorial Day & July 4th — Major sale events for outdoor power equipment
  • Prime Day in July — Amazon's best prices on Greenworks, Worx, and Sun Joe
