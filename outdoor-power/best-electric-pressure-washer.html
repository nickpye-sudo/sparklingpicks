💡 Electric vs Gas: Which Should You Choose?

Electric pressure washers are ideal for homeowners. They're quieter (65-75 dB vs 85+ dB for gas), require zero maintenance, and work perfectly for driveways, decks, siding, and vehicles. Gas models (2500-4000 PSI) are overkill for home use and risk damaging surfaces. For 95% of residential cleaning, 1600-2300 PSI electric is the sweet spot.

🛒 Pressure Washer Buying Guide

PSI vs GPM — What Actually Matters?

PSI (pounds per square inch) measures the force of the water stream. Higher PSI cuts through grime faster. GPM (gallons per minute) measures water volume — it determines how quickly you can rinse large areas.

For homeowner use, 1600-2300 PSI is the sweet spot. Under 1600 PSI struggles with concrete; over 2500 PSI risks damaging wood decks and paint.

Task-Based PSI Guide

  • 1200-1600 PSI: Cars, patio furniture, windows, bikes
  • 1600-2000 PSI: Decks, siding, fences, grills, outdoor furniture
  • 2000-2300 PSI: Driveways, concrete, heavy stains, prepping surfaces for paint
  • 2500+ PSI: Commercial/industrial — overkill for home use

Corded vs Battery: When to Go Cordless

Corded electric pressure washers are cheaper and never run out of power. Choose corded if you'll always be near an outlet.

Battery pressure washers like the Ego POWER+ offer freedom — no extension cords, no tripping hazards, no outlet hunting. They're ideal for: cleaning cars away from home, remote areas without outlets, and users invested in battery ecosystems. The tradeoff is cost and runtime.

What Accessories Matter?

  • Surface cleaner (flat attachment): Cleans driveways 3-4x faster than a wand. Worth $50-80 as an add-on.
  • Turbo nozzle: Rotating jet for stubborn stains. Most models include one.
  • Foam cannon: Creates thick soap foam for car washing. Some models include it; otherwise $20-30.
  • Extension wand: Reach second-story siding without a ladder. $30-50.

How Long Do Electric Pressure Washers Last?

Budget models ($100-150): 2-4 years with occasional use. Mid-range ($200-300): 4-6 years. Premium ($350+): 6-10+ years. Water-cooled motors (like the Kärcher K5) last the longest because they don't overheat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 2000 PSI enough to clean a driveway?

Yes, 2000 PSI handles most residential driveways. For heavily stained or oil-soaked concrete, 2300 PSI or a surface cleaner attachment speeds up the job significantly. Gas models (2500+ PSI) are overkill for typical driveways.

Can electric pressure washers damage wood decks?

Yes, if you use too much pressure or hold the nozzle too close. For wood decks, use 1200-1500 PSI with a 25° or 40° nozzle, held 12-18 inches from the surface. The 0° and 15° nozzles can gouge soft wood.

Do I need a special outlet for an electric pressure washer?

Most electric pressure washers run on standard 120V, 15A outlets — the same as any appliance. Heavy-duty models (2300+ PSI) may draw 15A continuously, so avoid using extension cords over 50 feet or you'll lose pressure. A dedicated 20A circuit is ideal for premium models.

How much water does an electric pressure washer use?

Electric pressure washers use 1.2-1.8 GPM — about 80% less water than a garden hose at the same cleaning time. A typical driveway cleaning uses 20-40 gallons vs 100+ gallons with a hose.
