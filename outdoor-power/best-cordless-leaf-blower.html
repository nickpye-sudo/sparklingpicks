Cordless Leaf Blower Buying Guide

CFM vs. MPH: What Matters More?

CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) measures air volume—how much debris you can move at once. Higher CFM means you can clear large piles of leaves faster.

MPH (Miles Per Hour) measures air speed—how forcefully you can dislodge stuck debris. Higher MPH is better for wet leaves and matted grass.

Our recommendation: For most homeowners, prioritize CFM above 500. MPH above 140 is sufficient for typical yard work.

Battery Voltage Explained

  • 40V: Entry-level. Good for small yards, light debris.
  • 56V: Sweet spot for most homeowners. Balances power and runtime.
  • 60V/80V: Near-commercial power. Best for large properties or heavy debris.

Runtime Considerations

Manufacturer runtime claims are often optimistic. Expect about 60-70% of advertised runtime under normal use. For yards over 1/2 acre, consider models with 70+ minute runtime or invest in a second battery.

Noise Levels

Check local ordinances—many suburbs restrict leaf blower use before 8 AM. Models under 65 dB are considered "quiet" and are less likely to disturb neighbors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can cordless leaf blowers match gas power?

Yes, modern 56V and 80V cordless blowers match or exceed the performance of most residential gas blowers. Commercial-grade cordless backpack models like the Ego LB7654 rival professional gas equipment while producing zero emissions.

How long do cordless blower batteries last?

Most batteries last 3-5 years with proper care. Store them at 40-60% charge in a climate-controlled space. Avoid leaving them on the charger indefinitely or letting them fully discharge.

Should I buy a blower/vacuum combo?

We generally recommend dedicated blowers over combos. Vacuum functions add weight, complexity, and potential failure points. A rake or separate mulcher is usually more practical for collecting leaves.

Is Ego or Greenworks better?

Ego offers superior build quality, longer runtime, and quieter operation. Greenworks provides more raw power per dollar. If budget is tight, go Greenworks. If you want the best overall experience, choose Ego.
