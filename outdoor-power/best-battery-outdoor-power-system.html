Complete Buying Guide

How to Choose Your Battery Platform

1. Assess Your Property

  • Under 1/4 acre: Any platform works. Go with Ryobi for budget or Ego for premium.
  • 1/4 to 1/2 acre: Ego 56V or Greenworks 80V recommended for adequate runtime.
  • 1/2 to 1 acre: Greenworks 80V or Ego with extra battery. Consider two-stage snow blowers.
  • Over 1 acre: Consider a zero-turn mower or supplement with riding mower. Greenworks/Ego recommended.

2. Consider Your Climate

  • Heavy snow regions: Ego has the best snow blower lineup. Greenworks and Kobalt also strong.
  • Year-round lawn care: Prioritize mower quality (Ego > Greenworks > Ryobi).
  • Lots of trees: Make sure the platform has a good chainsaw and pole saw.

3. Check Your Existing Tools

  • Already own DeWalt 20V MAX? FlexVolt 60V makes sense — batteries work with both.
  • Already own Milwaukee M18? M18 FUEL outdoor tools share batteries (but no mower).
  • Starting fresh? Ego or Greenworks for dedicated outdoor platforms.

4. Where Do You Shop?

  • Home Depot: Ryobi (exclusive), Ego, DeWalt, Milwaukee
  • Lowe's: Kobalt (exclusive), Ego, Greenworks
  • Amazon: Ego, Greenworks, DeWalt, Milwaukee (no Ryobi or Kobalt)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use batteries from one brand in another brand's tools?

No, battery platforms are not cross-compatible. Ego batteries only work with Ego tools, Ryobi with Ryobi, etc. This is why choosing the right platform upfront is so important — you're locked in once you invest in batteries.

Are battery outdoor tools as powerful as gas?

For most homeowner tasks, yes. Modern 56V-80V battery mowers, blowers, and trimmers match or exceed gas equivalents in performance. The main limitations are runtime (gas runs until empty) and extreme duty applications. For typical suburban yards, battery is now the better choice for most people.

How long do outdoor power batteries last?

Quality lithium batteries (Ego, DeWalt, Milwaukee) last 500-1000+ charge cycles before significant degradation. With typical homeowner use (weekly mowing, seasonal snow blowing), that's 8-15 years of useful life. Budget brands may degrade faster.

Should I buy tools as kits or bare tools?

Buy your first tool as a kit (includes battery + charger), then purchase additional tools as bare tools. Since batteries are interchangeable within a platform, you only need 2-3 batteries total to cover all your tools. This approach saves hundreds of dollars.
