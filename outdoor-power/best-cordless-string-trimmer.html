How to Choose a Cordless String Trimmer

Cutting Width

Wider = faster coverage. 12-13" is fine for small yards. 14-16" handles most residential properties efficiently. If you have an acre or more, go for 16"+.

Battery Voltage

Higher voltage generally means more power. 18V handles light trimming. 40V covers most residential needs. 56V+ rivals gas trimmers for heavy-duty work.

Runtime

Match runtime to your yard size. 30 minutes covers a small yard. 45-60 minutes handles most residential properties. For large properties, prioritize 60+ minutes or buy a spare battery.

Line Feed System

Bump-feed is traditional but can jam. Auto-feed systems (like Ego's Powerload) save frustration. Some trimmers use fixed-blade heads instead of line — more durable but less versatile.

Weight and Ergonomics

You'll be holding this for 30+ minutes. Under 8 lbs is comfortable for most users. Adjustable handles and shoulder straps help on heavier models.

Cordless vs. Gas String Trimmers

Modern cordless trimmers match gas performance for residential use. You lose the unlimited runtime of gas, but gain:

  • No fuel mixing — just charge and go
  • Zero emissions — no fumes in your face
  • Quiet operation — won't annoy neighbors at 7am
  • Low maintenance — no carburetors to clean
  • Instant start — push a button, done

For most homeowners, cordless is now the better choice. Only consider gas if you need to run continuously for hours without recharging.
