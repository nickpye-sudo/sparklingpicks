What Is a Sparkling Water Maker?

A sparkling water maker uses pressurized CO2 to carbonate plain tap water, creating sparkling water on demand. No electricity required — just a CO2 cylinder and the machine.

Key Benefits

  • Save money: ~$0.30 per liter vs $1.50+ for bottles
  • Reduce plastic: One reusable bottle replaces hundreds per year
  • Always fresh: Carbonate on demand, never flat
  • Convenience: No hauling heavy bottles from the store

How Does a Sparkling Water Maker Work?

  1. Fill the bottle with cold tap water (to the marked line)
  2. Insert the bottle into the machine
  3. Press/pull to carbonate — CO2 is injected into the water
  4. Remove and enjoy — fresh sparkling water in seconds

No electricity required. The CO2 cylinder provides the pressure. A single cylinder makes about 60 liters of sparkling water.
