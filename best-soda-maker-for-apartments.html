Best Soda Maker for Apartments

Compact options for small kitchens and limited counter space.

📅 Updated Feb 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Living in a small apartment doesn't mean you can't enjoy fresh sparkling water. But counter space is precious, so you need a soda maker that earns its spot. Here are the most compact options.

Storage Tips for Small Spaces

  • Keep one bottle, not three: Extra bottles take up space. One is enough for most solo/couple use.
  • Store cylinders vertically: CO2 cylinders can stand upright in a cabinet corner.
  • Consider counter vs cabinet: Aarke is worth leaving out; Terra might be better stored and pulled out when needed.
  • Skip the Duo: Glass bottles are elegant but the larger footprint isn't worth it in small kitchens.

Other Apartment Considerations

Noise

All soda makers make a hissing sound when carbonating — it's unavoidable. But it's brief (5-10 seconds) and not loud enough to disturb neighbors. No concerns here.

CO2 Cylinder Disposal

Empty cylinders need to be exchanged, not thrown away. Most major retailers offer exchanges. In dense urban areas, finding an exchange point is usually easy.

Delivery Considerations

CO2 cylinders are considered hazardous goods and may have shipping restrictions. Buying in-store is often easier than delivery for exchanges.

Our Recommendation

For apartments: Aarke if you can afford it (beautiful enough to display, smallest footprint), Terra if you're on a budget (nearly as compact, excellent value).

Skip the Duo — it's larger and the glass bottles are heavier and more fragile, which matters when storage is limited.
