What You Need to Know

For home backup, you need at least 1,500Wh capacity to run essential appliances during an outage:

  • Refrigerator: ~150W continuous, 1,200Wh/day
  • Wi-Fi router: ~10W, 240Wh/day
  • Phone charging: ~15W per device
  • LED lights: ~10W per bulb
  • CPAP machine: ~30-60W overnight

A 2,000Wh power station can run your fridge, router, and charge devices for 12-24 hours depending on usage.

Our Top Picks

What About Gas Generators?

Gas generators cost less upfront (~$500-800 for 2,000W) but have major downsides:

  • ❌ Noisy: 60-70dB (like a vacuum cleaner running constantly)
  • ❌ Fumes: Must stay outside; CO poisoning risk
  • ❌ Fuel: Need to store gasoline; runs out
  • ❌ Maintenance: Oil changes, carburetor cleaning

Portable power stations are silent, indoor-safe, and maintenance-free. They cost more upfront but are far more convenient for typical 4-24 hour outages.

How We Tested

We evaluated each power station on:

  • Run time: How long powering a 150W load (simulated fridge)
  • Recharge speed: Time from 0-100% via wall outlet
  • Output quality: Pure sine wave verification
  • Build quality: Materials, ports, display clarity
  • Warranty & support: Length and responsiveness
