What We Like

  • Massive capacity: 3,600Wh base — runs a home for 24+ hours
  • Real whole-home backup: Can integrate with home panel via Smart Home Panel
  • 3,600W output: Powers AC units, dryers, well pumps
  • EcoFlow ecosystem: Best app, best support

What We Don't Like

  • Heavy: 99 lbs — needs wheels
  • Expensive: $2,699 base; expansion adds up fast
  • Overkill for most: Most people don't need this much

Delta Pro vs Delta 2 Max

  • Delta 2 Max: 2,048Wh, $1,699 — enough for most people
  • Delta Pro: 3,600Wh, $2,699 — for power-hungry homes or serious preppers

Unless you need to power high-draw appliances (AC, well pump), the Delta 2 Max is the smarter buy.

Who Should Buy

The Delta Pro is for serious home backup — people who want to power their entire home during outages, or who are building a solar + battery system. For camping and occasional backup, get the Delta 2 Max.
