How We Define "Budget"

We split budget power stations into two tiers:

  • Under $300: 200-300Wh, ideal for phones, tablets, laptops, small devices
  • Under $500: 500-700Wh, can run small appliances, CPAP machines, more devices

💡 Value Metric: Cost per Wh

We measure value as dollars per watt-hour. Lower is better:

  • Bluetti EB3A: $0.74/Wh (best value)
  • Anker 521: $0.86/Wh
  • Jackery 300 Plus: $0.86/Wh
  • Bluetti EB70S: $0.63/Wh (best value in $500 tier)

💰 Under $300: Best for Basics

These power stations handle phone charging, laptops, cameras, and small electronics. Perfect for weekend camping or as a backup for essentials.

1. Jackery Explorer 300 Plus — Best Under $300

The 300 Plus is Jackery's newest compact station. At 288Wh and only 7.8 lbs, it's incredibly portable. LiFePO4 battery means 3,000+ cycles — it'll last for years.

Powers: Smartphones (20+ charges), laptops (4-5 charges), tablets, cameras, drones

Best for: Camping, travel, everyday backup

2. Anker 521 PowerHouse — Best Value Under $250

Anker (famous for phone chargers) makes a solid entry-level power station. 256Wh capacity, LiFePO4 battery, and Anker's 5-year warranty. The app is basic but functional.

Powers: Phones, tablets, laptops, small fans

Best for: Budget-conscious buyers who want reliability

3. Bluetti EB3A — Cheapest Name-Brand Option

At $199, the EB3A is the most affordable power station from a major brand. 268Wh capacity with 600W output (1,200W surge). Includes wireless charging pad on top.

Powers: Phones, tablets, small appliances

Best for: First-time buyers, basic needs

💰💰 Under $500: More Power, More Value

Step up to 500-700Wh and you can run small appliances, CPAP machines, and charge multiple devices for days.

4. Jackery Explorer 500 — Reliable Workhorse

The Explorer 500 (518Wh) is one of the best-selling power stations ever made. Not the newest tech, but proven reliability and excellent customer support.

Powers: CPAP (1-2 nights), mini fridge (5-6 hours), laptops (6-8 charges)

Best for: CPAP backup, camping, light emergency use

5. Bluetti EB70S — Best Value Under $500

The EB70S offers 716Wh for $449 — that's $0.63/Wh, the best value on this list. LiFePO4 battery (3,500+ cycles), 800W output, and solid build quality.

Powers: CPAP (2-3 nights), mini fridge (8-10 hours), multiple devices

Best for: Maximum value per dollar

What to Avoid

  • No-name Amazon brands: Specs are often fake, no warranty support
  • Modified sine wave: Can damage sensitive electronics (CPAP, laptops)
  • Anything under $100: Too small to be useful; likely poor quality

Budget vs Premium: Is It Worth Spending More?

Budget power stations have trade-offs:

  • Slower charging: 4-6 hours vs 1-2 hours for premium
  • Basic apps: Less monitoring and control
  • Less capacity: Need to manage power more carefully

But for occasional use (camping, short outages), budget models deliver 80% of the value at 40% of the price.

The Bottom Line

Under $300: Get the Jackery Explorer 300 Plus ($249). Best balance of capacity, portability, and build quality.

Under $500: Get the Bluetti EB70S ($449). Best value per watt-hour, LiFePO4 battery, and enough capacity for serious use.
