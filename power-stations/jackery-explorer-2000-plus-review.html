What We Like

  • Massive expansion: Add up to 5 battery packs for 12,000Wh total
  • LiFePO4 battery: 4,000 cycles = 10+ years of daily use
  • 1,200W solar input: Highest in class
  • Jackery quality: Best customer support in the industry
  • Wheels included: Roll it around, don't lift it

What We Don't Like

  • Price: $1,999 is steep; expansion batteries add up

  • Slow AC charging: 2 hours (vs 1 hour for EcoFlow)

Expansion Options

  • Base unit: 2,042Wh — $1,999
  • + 1 battery: 4,084Wh — ~$3,500
  • + 5 batteries: 12,000Wh — ~$8,000

Who Should Buy

The Explorer 2000 Plus is for serious preppers and full-time RVers who want a system that grows with their needs. If you just need backup for occasional outages, the EcoFlow Delta 2 is better value.
