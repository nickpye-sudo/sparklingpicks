Real-World Use Cases

🚐 Van Life / RV Living

This is where the AC200MAX shines. We tested it in a camper van setup:

  • Daily load: 12V fridge (50W avg), laptop, phones, LED lights, water pump
  • Total consumption: ~600Wh/day
  • Runtime on base unit: 3+ days without charging
  • With 400W solar: Indefinite (generates more than we used)

Verdict: Perfect. The 900W solar input means you can connect serious panels and stay topped up even on cloudy days.

🏠 Home Emergency Backup

We simulated a power outage running essential loads:

  • Loads: Full-size fridge, Wi-Fi router, phone charging, LED lights
  • Combined draw: ~100W average
  • Runtime: 18+ hours on base unit

Note: The 20ms switchover means it's NOT a true UPS — don't use it for servers or medical equipment that needs instant failover. For fridges and general home use, 20ms is unnoticeable.

🏕️ Extended Camping (Off-Grid)

Week-long backcountry trip with no grid access:

  • Setup: AC200MAX + 200W portable solar panel
  • Daily use: Phones, camera batteries, drone, portable projector for movies
  • Result: Never dropped below 40% battery

The high solar input capacity is the difference-maker here. Most competitors cap at 200-400W; the AC200MAX accepts 900W, so you can recharge in 2-3 hours of good sun.

🔌 Running High-Wattage Appliances

Our take:

  • Choose AC200MAX if: You want more capacity per dollar, care about portability (62 vs 99 lbs), and don't need home electrical panel integration.
  • Choose Delta Pro if: You want maximum capacity (25kWh expandable), home backup with smart panel integration, or need 3,600W output for larger loads.

For most RV, camping, and moderate home backup needs, the AC200MAX delivers 80% of the Delta Pro's capability at 57% of the price.
