Why It's Great for Solar

  • 900W solar input: Highest in its class — charge in 2-3 hours of sun
  • Dual charging: Solar + AC simultaneously
  • LiFePO4: Handles temperature extremes, 3,500+ cycles
  • Expandable: Add B230/B300 batteries for up to 8,192Wh

What We Don't Like

  • Heavy: 62 lbs is serious weight
  • Slow AC charging: 3.5 hours (vs 1.5 for EcoFlow)
  • Basic app: Functional but not polished

AC200MAX vs EcoFlow Delta 2 Max

Bluetti wins: Solar input (900W vs 500W), LiFePO4 battery, $100 cheaper

EcoFlow wins: Faster AC charging, better app, lighter weight

Who Should Buy

If you're building a solar-powered setup — RV, off-grid cabin, van life — the AC200MAX is the clear choice. If you mainly charge from wall outlets, get the EcoFlow Delta 2 Max.
