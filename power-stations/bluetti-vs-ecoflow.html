Battery Chemistry: Why It Matters

The biggest difference between Bluetti and EcoFlow is battery chemistry:

LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate) — Bluetti's Standard

  • 3,500+ charge cycles (vs 800 for standard lithium-ion)
  • Safer (less prone to thermal runaway)
  • Works better in extreme temperatures
  • Heavier per Wh

NCM/NCA (Standard Lithium-ion) — EcoFlow's Legacy

  • 800-1,000 charge cycles
  • Lighter weight
  • Higher energy density
  • Faster charging possible

Note: EcoFlow's newer models (Delta 2, River 2 series) use LFP batteries, closing the gap with Bluetti.
