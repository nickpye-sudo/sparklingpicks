Buying Guide: How to Choose

How much capacity do you need?

  • 300-500Wh: Phones, laptops, LED lights, small devices
  • 500-1000Wh: Above + mini fridge, CPAP machine, drone charging
  • 1000-2000Wh: Above + power tools, electric coolers, multiple days off-grid
  • 2000Wh+: Whole-home backup, extended off-grid living

Key Specs Explained

  • Wh (Watt-hours): Total energy capacity. Higher = more runtime.
  • W (Watts): Maximum power output. Must exceed your device's needs.
  • LiFePO4: Newer battery chemistry with 3000+ cycles (vs 500 for Li-ion).

Our Recommendation

For most people, the Jackery Explorer 1000 Plus offers the best balance. Enough capacity for real-world use, portable enough to move, and expandable if your needs grow.

On a budget? The Jackery 500 is a fantastic starting point.
