How Solar Generators Work

  1. Solar panel converts sunlight to electricity
  2. Power station stores it in battery
  3. Use stored power anytime via AC outlets/USB

How to Size Your Solar Generator

  • Laptop + small devices: 500-1,000Wh + 100-200W panel
