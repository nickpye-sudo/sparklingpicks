🗓️ Best Times to Buy

Power station prices follow predictable seasonal patterns. Here's when to expect the best deals:

🏆 Prime Day (July)

Best deals of the year. Expect 25-40% off major brands.

🏆 Black Friday (Nov)

Nearly as good. 20-35% off, sometimes better bundles.

Spring Sales (Mar-Apr)

Camping season prep. 10-20% off portable models.

Labor Day (Sept)

End of summer clearance. 15-25% off.
