Quick Verdict

The Jackery Explorer 300 Plus is exactly what we've been asking Jackery to make for years: a compact power station with modern features. It swaps the older NMC battery for LFP chemistry (3,000 cycles vs 500), adds USB-C Power Delivery, and charges from wall in just 2 hours.

At 7.8 lbs with 288Wh capacity, it's the perfect size for day trips, weekend camping, or keeping in your car for emergencies. The 300W AC output won't power a space heater, but it handles everything else you'd reasonably want in this size class: laptops, drones, CPAP machines, phones, cameras, and mini fridges.

Our recommendation: If you want a compact, grab-and-go power station that will last a decade, this is it. The Explorer 300 Plus represents Jackery catching up to (and in some ways surpassing) the competition. At $299, it's an easy recommendation.

Real-World Testing

📱 Phone Charging

The Explorer 300 Plus charged an iPhone 15 Pro from 0-100% approximately 14 times before depleting. USB-C PD delivered fast charging speeds (0-50% in under 30 minutes). For a weekend camping trip, that's more than enough for a family of four.

💻 MacBook Air M2

Running a MacBook Air M2 at moderate brightness with light work, we got approximately 5 hours of runtime. The USB-C PD port can also charge the MacBook at full speed (67W) — no adapter needed.

🎮 Nintendo Switch

Charged a Nintendo Switch approximately 8 times. The USB-C port delivers power while playing, so you can game indefinitely (until the 300 Plus runs out, anyway).

💨 CPAP Machine

Running a ResMed AirSense 10 (without humidifier), we got approximately 5 nights of 8-hour sessions. With the humidifier on, that drops to about 2 nights. Good for short camping trips.

📷 Camera Batteries

Charged Sony NP-FZ100 batteries approximately 20+ times via USB-C. Perfect for photographers who need reliable power in the field.

💡 Pro Tip: USB-C Pass-Through

The 100W USB-C port works bidirectionally. You can charge the Explorer 300 Plus with a USB-C PD charger (if you forgot your wall adapter) or use it as a 100W output for laptops. Extremely versatile.
