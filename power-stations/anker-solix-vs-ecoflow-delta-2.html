The Bottom Line

🏆 Winner: Anker Solix C1000

The Anker Solix C1000 delivers better value: it's $100 cheaper, charges faster (58 min vs 80 min to 80%), has a longer warranty (5 years vs 3), and offers higher USB-C output (140W vs 100W). The EcoFlow Delta 2 has slightly higher AC output (1,800W vs 1,600W), but for most users, the Anker wins on the metrics that matter.

When to Choose Each

📱 Charging Devices & Laptops

The Anker's 140W USB-C ports charge MacBook Pros and iPads faster than EcoFlow's 100W ports.

Pick: Anker Solix C1000

⚡ Running High-Wattage Appliances

The Delta 2's 1,800W output handles larger appliances like space heaters or microwaves more comfortably.

Pick: EcoFlow Delta 2

🏕️ Camping & Outdoor Use

Both work well. The Anker charges faster from solar panels (up to 600W input), which matters off-grid.

Pick: Anker Solix C1000

🔋 Maximum Expandability

If you might need 3,000Wh+, the Delta 2 supports two expansion batteries vs Anker's one.

Pick: EcoFlow Delta 2

Charging Speed: Anker Wins

The Anker Solix C1000 uses 1,200W AC charging (upgradable to 1,600W with HyperFlash), reaching 80% in under an hour. The EcoFlow Delta 2 charges at 1,200W but takes 80 minutes to hit 80%.

For solar charging, both accept up to 500W input, though the Anker's MPPT controller is slightly more efficient in our testing.

Build Quality & Warranty

Both units feel solid. The Anker has a slightly more premium finish with a soft-touch coating and integrated LED light. The EcoFlow has a rubberized handle that's comfortable but picks up dust.

The real difference: Anker offers a 5-year warranty out of the box. EcoFlow gives you 3 years, extendable to 5 if you register — but many buyers forget.

Final Verdict

For most buyers, the Anker Solix C1000 is the better choice. It's $100 less, charges faster, has better USB-C specs, and comes with a longer warranty. The EcoFlow Delta 2 only makes sense if you need the extra AC output headroom or plan to expand to 3,000Wh+ with multiple batteries.
